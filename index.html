Reading-comprehension annotation task

You'll read short stories or conversations, a question about them, and a model's step-by-step reasoning toward an answer. For each one you'll answer a single yes/no/can't-tell question about the reasoning itself — not about whether the model's final answer was right or wrong.

There are three kinds of question, grouped into blocks so you're not switching between them constantly. A short calibration set comes first, with the intended answer shown afterward so you can check your reading against it — those don't count toward the real data.

Expect roughly 1–2 hours total, and you can close the tab and come back later; your progress is saved in this browser.

Calibration (practice)

You'll see practice items. Answer each one as best you can, then the intended answer and a short explanation will appear. These are for your own calibration only — they are not scored or used in the analysis.
